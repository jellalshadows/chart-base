# 1.4.0: a GRPCRoute, extra HTTPRoute rules and `appProtocol`

1.4.0 adds three optional keys. They are the parts of the 0.11.0 that was cut that can be added without breaking
anything. Values without them render exactly as with 1.3.1.

## What changed

- **`grpcRoute`** renders a Gateway API GRPCRoute (`gateway.networking.k8s.io/v1`) to the component's Service, alone
  or next to the HTTPRoute ([ADR-0069](../adr/0069-grpcroute-httproute-rules-and-appprotocol.md), amending ADR-0016,
  ADR-0040, ADR-0048 and ADR-0064). It takes:
  - `parentRefs`;
  - `hostnames`;
  - closed `matches` (`method` and `headers`; empty: every request);
  - `port`, a `ports` entry by name (`null`: the first).
- **`httpRoute.rules`** adds rules after the one `httpRoute.matches` makes, each with its `matches` and the `ports`
  entry it sends to. The first rule keeps its frozen shape.
- **`ports[].appProtocol`** goes on the Service port only, for example `kubernetes.io/h2c` for a cleartext gRPC backend.
- **Guards:** the render fails on any of these:
  - a rule without matches;
  - a `port` that names no `ports` entry;
  - a GRPCRoute without `parentRefs`;
  - a GRPCRoute without `service.enabled`.

  The schema rejects a GRPCRoute on a CronJob or a Job.
- **NetworkPolicy:** a GRPCRoute counts as a route. With `networkPolicy` on, it needs a source, and
  `routeTrafficAllowedElsewhere` has an effect with it. The `routeTrafficAllowedElsewhere`-without-a-route message now
  lists `grpcRoute`.
- **Optional** ([ADR-0064](../adr/0064-from-1-0-0-a-new-key-is-optional.md)): `null` and an absent key render the
  default. The guards are template guards, not a schema `if`. An `if` on an absent `grpcRoute`, which is what a
  `--reuse-values` upgrade produces, would hold vacuously.

## Evidence

- **Unit tests:** 1512 (31 new), written test-first, unit by unit. They cover every new key with `null`, the guards,
  and the schema rejections at their paths.
- **Snapshot:** the `full` scenario gains the GRPCRoute, the extra HTTPRoute rule and `appProtocol: http`.
- **Differential:** the seven `ci/` scenarios of 1.3.1 render identically with the 1.3.1 chart and the 1.4.0 chart,
  on Helm 4.3.0 and 3.22.0 (14 renders).
- **The `--reuse-values` shape:** the 1.4.0 chart, rendered with 1.0.0's and 1.3.1's `values.yaml` as defaults, renders
  all seven `ci/` scenarios on Helm 4.3.0 and 3.22.0.
- **Independent verification:** no blocker; 5 of 5 claims pass over 1,734 probes and 7,130 renders.
  - Values that 1.3.1 accepts render the same.
  - The NetworkPolicy guard matrix is unchanged while `grpcRoute` is absent or disabled, and a GRPCRoute without a
    source always fails.
  - kubeconform found 83 of 83 rendered resources valid.
  - The contract gained 36 keys and lost none.
- **Native review** (risk, readability, reliability): approved with no blocking finding. The follow-ups are recorded
  in the pull request.
- **e2e on kind 1.33 and 1.37:** the API server stores the `full` scenario's GRPCRoute, its extra HTTPRoute rule and
  the Service port's `appProtocol`. The release has 18 objects.
- **Known, left to the "`null` means absent" release:** on a `--reuse-values` upgrade from a release older than
  1.4.0, an explicit `null` on `grpcRoute`, `grpcRoute.enabled` or `httpRoute.rules` reaches the schema and fails.
  `autoscaling.kedaHandover: null` behaves the same way on 1.3.1.
