# 1.5.0: a `null` means absent, chart-wide

1.5.0 adds no key. It treats a `null` as absent on a map entry or on an optional member of a closed object; 1.4.0
rejected those with `got null, want …`. Values that render on 1.4.0 render the same manifest.

## What changed

- **One rule, chart-wide** ([ADR-0070](../adr/0070-a-null-means-absent-chart-wide.md), amending ADR-0045, ADR-0049,
  ADR-0050, ADR-0051, ADR-0054, ADR-0055, ADR-0059 and ADR-0064). A `null` is now absent in:
  - the main container's `env.<NAME>`;
  - a source or an optional field inside any `valueFrom`;
  - a key of `config` and of the label, annotation and selector maps (a common value for that key then applies);
  - a `lifecycle` hook or handler;
  - `maxSurge` and `maxUnavailable`;
  - a `dnsConfig` field;
  - `ports[].servicePort` (the `containerPort` is used instead);
  - a direction or a field of a `behavior`.
- **An inherited `externalSecretKeyRef`:** an environment that disables the ExternalSecret can now remove it or switch
  its source from an overlay, with `env.<NAME>: null` or with `externalSecretKeyRef: null` next to another source.
- **Keys added after 1.0.0** (`autoscaling.kedaHandover`, `grpcRoute`, `httpRoute.rules`) take `null` as their
  default. That covers a `--reuse-values` upgrade from 1.0.x–1.3.x, the case 1.4.0's record left open.
- **What keeps its rule:**
  - required fields;
  - a `valueFrom` or a hook whose every member is `null`;
  - reserved label and annotation keys;
  - a `null` inside a list;
  - pass-through objects, which still render the `null`.
- **One message changed:** the main container's `externalSecretKeyRef` remedy now ends `(an overlay removes it with
  env.<NAME>: null)`, as an entry's does.
- **Mechanism:** each nullable value is wrapped as `{"if": {"not": {"type": "null"}}, "then": <1.4.0 schema>}`, so every
  non-null error keeps its 1.4.0 text. Templates and guards read one pruned copy of the values.

## Evidence

- **Unit tests:** 1582 (70 new), written test-first, unit by unit. The 1.4.0 suite passes 1505 of its 1512 tests against
  the 1.5.0 templates. Each of the 7 failures asserts a `got null` rejection that is now intended, or the old remedy
  text.
- **CI alias contract:** in all four values layers, the check now expects the `null` cases to be absent. It passes on
  Helm 4.3.0 and 3.22.0 (59 checks), and fails against the 1.4.0 chart at the first new `null` check.
- **Independent verification:** no blocker; 6 of 6 claims pass on Helm 4.3.0 and 3.22.0.
  - Values that 1.4.0 renders give byte-identical output: 362 of 362 renders, normal shape and the `--reuse-values`
    shape from 1.0.0 and 1.3.1.
  - Non-null rejections are unchanged: 288 of 292 comparisons are identical, and the other 4 differ only by the
    intended message.
  - Unwrapping the null wrappers gives 1.4.0's schema exactly.
  - Secret references behave as expected in 46 cases on each Helm version.
  - kubeconform: 210 of 210 resources valid.
  - The contract keeps 689 keys.
- **Native review**, in three slices, because the whole change exceeded the reviewer context budget:
  - **Env:** approved.
  - **Maps and members:** approved.
  - **Post-1.0 keys, docs and the alias contract:** escalated. Its reviewers saw only that slice's diff, and concluded
    that the rule was not implemented. It was implemented in the two earlier slices, which form that slice's base. The
    alias contract passes all 59 checks at the slice's tip.
  - The follow-ups are recorded in the pull request.
- **Known, left as a follow-up:** a `null` post-1.0 key under `--reuse-values` is checked by scripts outside CI, not by
  a CI job.
