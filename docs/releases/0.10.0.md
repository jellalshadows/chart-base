# 0.10.0: execution record

This record keeps what happened while 0.10.0 was designed, built and reviewed: the process, the decisions and the
rulings with their reasons, the deviations from the plan, every review finding and what became of it, and the
measurements that changed a decision. It is written for a reader who never saw the working files of the execution;
those files are named here by their role (the research, the design, the design critique, the plan, the plan review, the
plan changes, the prototype report, the prototype's measurement logs, the execution ledger, the task reviews and the
final reviews), not by their location.

Roles: the **owner** of the repository authorized the execution on 2026-10-03 to run without asking, merges and
releases included (the autonomous mode); the **controller** wrote the design, dispatched every task and review, and
ruled on every finding; the **prototyper** built the prototype and the plan; an **implementer** applied one task of
the plan; a **reviewer** checked one task, the plan or the whole branch, on copies of the repository.

## What 0.10.0 is

The chart's HorizontalPodAutoscaler gains `autoscaling.behavior` (Kubernetes' `spec.behavior`, closed at every level
and validated, no `tolerance`) and `autoscaling.metrics` (Kubernetes' list, validated per type; a non-empty list is the
whole list and replaces the built-in targets, which stay 0.8.0's for an empty one). A Deployment component can instead
scale with a KEDA ScaledObject: the `keda` block (`minReplicaCount`, 1 by default, and `maxReplicaCount`, both
required, the polling and cooldown periods, `fallback`, `behavior`, and `triggers`, a map keyed by the trigger's name
whose `null` entries, optional fields and metadata keys are absent), exclusive with the HPA. No `spec.replicas` is
rendered while either autoscaler is on. Guards check what KEDA's admission webhook and operator would reject, the
containers a metric names, and 0.7.0's claim guard reads `keda.maxReplicaCount`, each with its remedy
([ADR-0055](../adr/0055-hpa-behavior-and-metrics-are-validated-pass-throughs.md),
[ADR-0056](../adr/0056-keda-scaledobject-a-curated-block-with-a-triggers-map.md),
[upgrade notes](../upgrading.md#09x--0100)).

## The release type: `feat:`, not breaking

- **Every key 0.10.0 adds was a schema error on `main`** (the root and `autoscaling` are closed), and `keda` is not in
  the root `required` list: every reader takes a missing block or `keda: null` as KEDA off.
- **Byte identity, measured:** the six `ci/` scenarios of `main`, `full` with its sidecar removed, the seven shared test
  values files and the 18 values blocks of `main`'s README recipes render the same manifests with 0.10.0 on Helm 4.3.0
  and 3.22.0 (the prototype's replay; the plan review and lens 2: 77 of 78 renders identical, the 78th an overlay block
  that fails alone on both charts with the same errors in another order; lens 1: 6 of 6, 7 of 7, 18 of 18). The fix wave
  rendered the seven `ci/` scenarios after each of its commits: byte-identical to the last task's commit on both Helm
  versions.
- **`--reuse-values`, measured with real upgrades:** the prototype, the plan review (10 upgrades) and lens 2 upgraded
  releases installed from the published 0.9.0 on kube-apiserver 1.37.0 with Helm 4.3.0 and 3.22.0: direct installs with
  and without the HPA and a sidecar, and umbrellas. Every upgrade exited 0 and revision 2 rendered revision 1's manifest
  byte for byte (measured with the branch's `Chart.yaml`, still at 0.9.0: the published 0.10.0 also changes the
  `helm.sh/chart` label in every object's metadata, never in a pod template,
  [ADR-0010](../adr/0010-chart-version-never-restarts-pods.md)). Skipped versions (0.4.x to 0.8.x) fail with the lines
  of 0.9.0's upgrade notes, as before.
- **The one documented failure:** turning KEDA on in a direct install upgraded with `--reuse-values` fails with `at
  '/keda': missing properties 'minReplicaCount', 'maxReplicaCount'` (the reused values are 0.9.x's, without a `keda`
  block, so the chart's defaults are not read) and writes no revision; writing both counts in that upgrade, or
  `--reset-then-reuse-values`, upgrades; an umbrella is not affected (measured by the plan review and lens 2). The
  upgrade guide states it (plan review C1).

## Measurements that changed a decision

- **KEDA's CRD stores what the chart renders, and kubeconform's pinned catalog does not catch five invalid
  ScaledObjects** (`maxReplicaCount: 0`, `minReplicaCount: -1`, `pollingInterval: 0`, `authenticationRef.kind: Secret`,
  the minimum above the maximum), which the CRD rejects: the chart's schema and guards reject them before a render.
- **KEDA's operator drops a `useCachedMetrics: false` at its first reconcile:** the chart renders the field only when
  `true` (judgment call 6), so an apply shows no drift.
- **KEDA's webhook and operator, rule by rule** (`useCachedMetrics: true` on `cron` and `memory` denied, `fallback` with
  only `cpu` denied, `cpu` alone at zero denied, `Utilization` on another trigger not Ready, `cpu` without
  `metricType` not Ready, `cooldownPeriod` and `pollingInterval` next to a minimum of 1 or more only warned, a
  55-character name denied, `containerName` never checked): a guard for each of these rules, but `pollingInterval`
  (it also sets how often KEDA refreshes the conditions), with the 54-character bound tied to KEDA's 63-character HPA
  name (judgment call 3).
- **One upgrade from the HPA to KEDA is denied by the webhook** and leaves the release `failed`; `kubectl delete hpa`
  first keeps the count; the two-upgrade path drops the count to 1 twice: the critique made `kubectl delete hpa` the
  documented primary path (A3), and no KEDA annotation is put on the chart's HPA (decision 9).
- **Helm 4's conflict on `spec.replicas`** happens only when the count changed through the scale subresource while Helm
  still owned the field: the upgrade guide states that, narrower than the design's wording (judgment call 14).
- **Helm 4 `--wait` returns in 0 to 1 s, exit 0, for a new or changed invalid ScaledObject** (the research): the e2e
  waits for `Ready` explicitly (judgment call 20), and the fix wave corrected two documents that read `--wait` as a
  Ready gate (lens 1 I-2).
- **Restricted Secret access in KEDA** (`KEDA_RESTRICT_SECRET_ACCESS`) makes a `...FromEnv` parameter fail: the docs
  point to a ClusterTriggerAuthentication then.
- **The `null` cases through an umbrella** (the alias contract, both Helm versions): Helm 4.3 drops a `null` of the
  umbrella's own values while nothing is passed for the alias (the counts keep their defaults, a `null` direction of
  `behavior` is dropped), Helm 3.22 keeps it: the docs state the rule per layer and per Helm version, and the fix wave
  extended it to any depth of `behavior` (lens 1 M-1).
- **The design's first remedy of the memory guard failed when applied alone:** the message leads with
  `targetMemoryUtilizationPercentage: null` (judgment call 2).
- **Raising `keda.maxReplicaCount` next to a `ReadWriteOnce` claim fails the claim guard** (the plan review): the
  fallback guard offers only to lower `fallback.replicas` there (S2). **Lowering the maximum alone next to a minimum or
  a fallback above 1 fails another guard** (the review of Task 4): the claim guard's remedy names them then (D-T4-I1).
- **The HPA controller never scales a target up from 0 while `minReplicas` is 1 or more** (Kubernetes source at
  v1.33.12 and v1.37.0; the e2e asserts it): the way back from KEDA at zero is documented with the order that works
  (A2), corrected by the fix wave (lens 1 I-1).
- **A native sidecar's usage is reported by the kubelet and metrics-server 0.9.0** (source reading): `ContainerResource`
  and `containerName` may name a sidecar, and the e2e installs metrics-server to assert it (judgment call 7).

## Deviations from the plan

- **Task 2:** the automatic restore of mutation T2.1 failed (the driver's working directory), and the implementer
  restored `templates/hpa.yaml` by hand. The reviewer re-ran T2.1 on its copy (exactly the two named tests failed) and
  the replay checker matched the tree. From Task 3 on, implementers were told to back files up with absolute paths.
- **The final lenses** ran in parallel with the last task review (a ruling above).
- **The fix wave**, where its brief left a choice or was narrower than the finding's class:
  - D-T6-M1: every `| head` pipeline of `alias-contract.sh` became a here-string (the new one and three of earlier
    releases: two of 0.9.0's, one of 0.1.0's), not only the new one; the other `echo ... | grep -q` lines of earlier
    releases, outside the D-T6 findings, were left.
  - D-T4-M1: the four missing violations cannot share one values set (a minimum of 0 excludes a minimum above the
    maximum), so the old test was retitled to what it holds and two disabled tests were added.
  - D-T7-M1: the `tolerance` sentence was labelled in `values.yaml` too.
  - `templates/hpa.yaml`'s comment "the schema ... rejects a null inside" was left: it is true of the schema, and Helm
    4.3 drops the umbrella's own `null` before the schema runs.
  - One harness error, no effect on the repository: the wave's first remedy-render script called Helm 3.22 through a
    PATH that Python on Windows does not search, so its "3.22" column was Helm 4.3. Found when a render contradicted the
    alias contract; the scripts then called each binary by its path and printed its version, and the re-run gave the
    same results with the real Helm 3.22.0. The unit suite, the alias contract and the other checks were not affected.
